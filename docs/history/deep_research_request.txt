Research questions for the review:
1. How is catastrophic forgetting defined and measured in current LLM research?
2. What is the distinction between genuine knowledge loss, behavioral interference, task-alignment loss, prompt-format sensitivity, and “spurious forgetting”?
3. What has been established about LoRA versus full fine-tuning?
4. Which explanations have been investigated: low-rank constraints, frozen parameters, update magnitude, effective rank, spectral/intruder dimensions, gradient conflict, layer location, task similarity, and optimization dynamics?
5. Which mitigation families have been studied:
   - regularization and weight constraints;
   - knowledge distillation or KL anchoring;
   - replay, rehearsal, and synthetic replay;
   - LoRA/PEFT variants and subspace constraints;
   - orthogonal or gradient-projected updates;
   - layer freezing and selective fine-tuning;
   - model merging and task vectors;
   - multiple adapters, routing, and mixture-of-experts;
   - sparse memory or architectural expansion;
   - data selection and curriculum methods?
6. Which conclusions conflict across papers, and can those conflicts be explained by model scale, task order, dataset similarity, target-task learning, evaluation format, LoRA rank, trainable-parameter budget, or compute?
7. What are the strongest methods currently reported, and are their comparisons sufficiently controlled to call them state of the art?
8. Which research questions remain genuinely open or underexplored?
